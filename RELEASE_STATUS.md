## D14 scope retained

- Genuine authorization and monetary/resource accounting, unknown outcome/cost reconciliation and stop observation.
- Home command/dialogue/outcome/approval integration and source, provenance, disclosure and revocation.
- Standard Executor performing a real authorized API small change with native runtimes disabled, including required isolated execution and evidence.
- Initial E independent evaluation, limited adoption, actual use and result in another task, and revocation.
- Automatic selection, permitted configurations, quality, continuation and selection evidence; separate persona/model/depth/tool/runtime/billing contracts.
- Purpose-specific runtime admission, stop/restart behavior, planned update with dispatch-disabled health check and rollback/recovery.
- Actual seven-day elapsed retrieval, suitable extension contracts and publishable materials.

Synthetic tests, limited real route observations and earlier-version receipts cannot establish those conditions for a new version. Future office/game/voice/3D features and full implementations in three languages are not newly imposed initial acceptance conditions.
